## On the numbers

Every figure was traced back to the archive before it was used, and the ones that could be re-derived
were re-derived by running the code:

- `pytest -q` → **707 passed, 2 xfailed**
- `verify_claims.py` → order invariance across all 120 permutations (spread exactly 0), eleven callable
  invariant checks, the deployment gate refusing institutional and undeclared contexts, the consent
  ledger detecting a tampered history, nine refusing methods all refusing when called, and a linear
  write-then-consult path

Four figures in `P1`/`P2` turned out to be **stale** — superseded by Session 12 and never propagated
into those papers. The unified draft carries the corrected values and says so explicitly. Three further
gaps were found while assembling it. All are recorded as **OW-6** through **OW-9** in the
outstanding-work appendix; two are marked blocking for submission.

The most serious: **the benchmark scripts and `results.json` that produce the headline safety numbers
are not in the archive.** Those numbers are carried on the authority of the session reports and are
labelled as not re-derived. Recovering those scripts is the first thing to do.
